Autism is rising at an alarming rate, up 317% since 2000. Today, 1 in 36 children in the US is diagnosed with Autism Spectrum Disorder, and the societal cost is projected to hit $461 billion annually by 2025. But beyond the numbers are real families navigating daily challenges.

Enter IoT: a game-changer in autism care

From smart wearables that track stress levels to smart homes that create sensory-friendly spaces, IoT technologies are transforming how care is delivered: real-time alerts help caregivers prevent meltdowns, data-driven insights personalize therapy, home automation reduces caregiver burnout, and remote monitoring makes virtual support possible.

Every child is unique, so should their care

Autism is a spectrum for a reason, each child shows different traits, strengths and sensitivities. One-size-fits-all therapy doesn't work. IoT empowers caregivers and therapists with data to understand each child's world, creating truly personalized care journeys that adapt and grow with them. This is about more than tech, it's about meaningful impact.

Enterprise investment in IoT for autism care isn't just innovation; it's social responsibility. The time to act is now.
