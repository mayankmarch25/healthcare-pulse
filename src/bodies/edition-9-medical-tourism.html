What is medical tourism?

Traveling abroad for non-emergency treatment, combining healthcare, travel logistics and recovery into one journey. Patients are motivated by cost savings (procedures 70-90% below Western rates), quality of care, treatment availability, and holistic AYUSH options.

Affordability

More than 50 JCI-accredited hospitals and 1,400+ NABH-certified facilities instill global trust.

3

Destinations and what to watch

Chennai leads in cardiology, Bangalore in orthopedics, Mumbai in oncology, Delhi NCR in multi-specialty tertiary care, Hyderabad and Kolkata in fertility and neurosurgery. Travellers should mind regulatory variability across states, plan follow-up logistics for tele-consultations, and research accreditation. Future trends: tele-medicine pre-assessment, AI-driven diagnostics, and wellness-tourism integration.

India offers world-class care at a fraction of global costs, with accreditation, e-visas and holistic options simplifying the journey. Growth to 1 million+ patients and $50 billion+ by 2030 is within reach.
