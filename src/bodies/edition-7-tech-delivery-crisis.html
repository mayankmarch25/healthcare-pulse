By 2035, the US will face a shortage of 80,000+ physicians. Six in ten Americans have at least one chronic condition, the population is aging rapidly, and costs keep rising with minimal legislative momentum. Technology offers the clearest path forward.

Why technology is the game-changer

In the face of workforce shortages and rising demand: AI for clinical decision support and workflow automation; wearables and ingestibles for real-time patient data and early detection; and smart home tech to support aging in place and remote monitoring.

Shift from demand-based to need-based care

Current care is reactive, patients seek help after symptoms appear. Need-based care uses data to trigger interventions proactively, improving outcomes, lowering costs and supporting clinicians.

To meet the Quadruple Aim, better outcomes, lower cost, improved experience and equity, we must scale digital health now.
