140 million consultations later, eSanjeevani isn't just a telehealth app. It's the foundation of India's national hybrid healthcare model, and a sleeping giant of monetization potential for tech and health enterprises.

The engine of India's digital healthcare

Launched by the Ministry of Health, eSanjeevani delivers care to rural and urban populations via secure video. Doctor-to-doctor connects PHCs/CHCs to specialist hospitals for advice and referrals; doctor-to-patient enables public OPDs through web and mobile, bringing the doctor virtually home. Now integrated with ABDM and ABHA Health IDs, it powers a comprehensive ecosystem of e-prescriptions, digital records and AI-powered triage.

The rise of hybrid "phygital" healthcare

India's next wave is phygital, where virtual and in-person care converge: seamless video consults, doorstep diagnostic collection, instant e-prescriptions, AI-driven triage, home delivery of medicines, and escalation to in-person care as needed. Key players span public (eSanjeevani) and private (Practo, Apollo 24/7, Tata 1mg), plus emerging WhatsApp-enabled care and multilingual AI chatbots.
